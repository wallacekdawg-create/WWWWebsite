Window Cleaning FAQ

Straight answers
before you book.

How much does window washing cost?

Your price depends on how many windows you have and which package you pick: The Classic, The Deluxe, or The Works. Send your address and we get back to you with a flat price within one business day.

Does the price include inside and outside?

Our packages cover the exterior. Interior cleaning is an add-on, and we spell it out on your price so there are no surprises. Screens and RainAway are always included free.

Are you insured?

Yes. SAUCE Window Cleaning carries general liability insurance and is owner-run. You can read what past customers say in our Google reviews.

Do you serve Utah?

Yes. We serve Utah County (Provo, Orem, Lehi, and nearby) and Park City, plus Elko, Spring Creek, Carlin, and Battle Mountain in Nevada.

Can you clean tall or hard-to-reach windows?

Yes. We bring the equipment to reach high glass safely, including windows around 30 feet off the ground that most homeowners would rather not tackle themselves.

Do you offer recurring service for businesses?

Yes. Storefronts, offices, and retail locations can set up a recurring schedule so the glass always looks professional and you never have to think about it.

What if I'm not happy with a spot?

Tell us and we come back to fix it. A job isn't finished until you're satisfied.

How fast can I get on the schedule?

Send the form or call (775) 460-6004. We confirm your flat price and a date within one business day.
